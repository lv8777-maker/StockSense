## Getting Started

### Registration Options

The Maverick Loyalty App offers **two ways to register**:

#### Option 1: Email & Password Registration
1. Visit the landing page
2. Click **"Register with Email"**
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (must be at least 8 characters)
   - Phone Number (optional)
4. **Select Your Plan** - Choose from:
   - **Starter Plan** (100 points) - Entry tier
   - **Explorer Plan** (250 points) - Mid tier with enhanced benefits
   - **Champion Plan** (400 points) - Premium tier
   - **Elite Plan** (500 points) - VIP tier with maximum rewards
5. Click **"Complete Registration"**
6. You'll be automatically logged in and taken to your dashboard

#### Option 2: Phone Number (South African)
1. Visit the landing page
2. Click **"Login with Replit"**
3. Enter your South African phone number (format: +27XXXXXXXXX)
4. Complete the authentication process
5. Access your dashboard

### First Login
After successful registration, you'll arrive at your personalized dashboard showing:
- Your current tier status
- Total points balance
- Recent transactions
- Tier progress bar

## Understanding the Tier System

### The Four Tiers

**1. Starter (0-999 points)**
- Entry-level benefits
- Standard reward access
- Basic member perks

**2. Explorer (1,000-2,999 points)**
- Enhanced benefits
- Priority reward access
- Exclusive offers

**3. Champion (3,000-4,999 points)**
- Premium benefits
- Early access to new rewards
- Special campaign invitations

**4. Elite (5,000+ points)**
- VIP treatment
- Maximum reward values
- Exclusive elite-only benefits

### How Tiers Work
- Tiers are based on your **total points balance**
- When you redeem points, your tier may decrease if you drop below the threshold
- Select a higher plan during registration to start with more points
- Earn more points to unlock higher tiers and better benefits

## Earning Points

### Method 1: Upload Receipts (OCR Technology)

The app uses advanced OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to automatically scan receipts and award points!

**Step-by-Step:**
1. Click **"Submit Receipt"** in the navigation menu
2. Take a photo of your receipt or select an existing image
3. Click the upload area or drag & drop your receipt image
4. The system will automatically:
   - Extract text from the receipt
   - Identify purchase categories
   - Calculate points earned
   - Add points to your balance

**Point Categories:**
- **Airtime Purchases**: 1 point per R1 spent (minimum R100)
- **Accessories**: 
  - R0-R499: 100 points
  - R500-R999: 250 points
  - R1000+: 500 points
- **Mobile Plans**:
  - R0-R299: 100 points
  - R300-R599: 250 points
  - R600+: 500 points

**Tips for Best Results:**
- Ensure receipt is clearly visible and well-lit
- Capture the entire receipt including totals
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, JPEG
- Maximum file size: 10MB

### Method 2: Transaction Simulator (Demo/Testing)
1. Go to **Dashboard**
2. Find the **"Transaction Simulator"** section
3. Select a transaction type:
   - Airtime Purchase
   - Device Purchase
   - Plan Upgrade
4. Enter the amount
5. Click **"Simulate Transaction"**
6. Points are instantly added to your account

### Method 3: Plan Selection/Upgrade
- Initial registration plan selection awards points immediately
- Upgrade to higher plans for bonus points

## Redeeming Rewards

### Browse Available Rewards
1. Click **"Rewards Catalog"** in the navigation
2. View all available rewards with:
   - Reward name and description
   - Points required
   - Category (e.g., Discounts, Gift Cards, Exclusive Experiences)
   - Stock availability

### Redeem a Reward
1. Find the reward you want
2. Check you have enough points
3. Click the **"Redeem"** button
4. Confirm the redemption
5. You'll receive:
   - A **unique redemption code** (save this!)
   - Confirmation message
   - Points deducted from your balance

### View Your Redemptions
1. Go to **"Purchase History"**
2. Switch to the **"Redemptions"** tab
3. See all your redeemed rewards with:
   - Redemption date
   - Reward name
   - Points used
   - Unique redemption code (for claiming the reward)

**Important:** Save your redemption codes! You'll need them to claim your rewards at Maverick locations or partner stores.

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot Upload Receipt
- Check image file size (max 10MB)
- Ensure image format is JPG, PNG, or JPEG
- Verify receipt is clear and readable
- Check internet connection

### Points Not Updating
- Refresh the page
- Check transaction history for confirmation
- Wait a few moments for system processing
- Contact support if issue persists

### Redemption Code Not Working
- Verify you copied the complete code
- Check expiration date of reward
- Ensure code hasn't been used already
- Contact Maverick support for assistance

### Login Issues
- Verify email/phone number is correct
- Check password (case-sensitive)
- Use "Forgot Password" if needed
- Clear browser cache and try again
