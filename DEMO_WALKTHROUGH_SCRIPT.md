## 💡 Handling Client Questions

### "How secure is the password storage?"
> "We use bcrypt hashing with 10 salt rounds - the same security standard used by banks and major tech companies. Passwords are never stored in plain text."

### "What if someone tries to hack the system?"
> "We have rate limiting on all sensitive endpoints - login attempts are limited to 5 per hour, registration to 10 per 15 minutes. This prevents brute force attacks and abuse."

### "Can it handle high volumes?"
> "The app is built on a modern, scalable stack with PostgreSQL database, React frontend, and Express backend. It's designed to handle thousands of concurrent users."

### "What about mobile users?"
> "The app is mobile-first. It works perfectly on iOS and Android browsers, with features like camera integration for receipt uploads optimized for mobile use."

### "How does the OCR work?"
> "We use Tesseract.js OCR engine to extract text from receipt images. It handles various formats, currencies, and can parse complex receipts with multiple items. The system automatically categorizes purchases and calculates points."
